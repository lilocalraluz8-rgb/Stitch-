About Us

Get to know Lilo(C) & Stitch Studios

Our Business

Lilo(C) & Stitch Studios is a handmade crochet business created with creativity, passion, and care. The name of the business has a special meaning. The owner, Ms. Alraluz L. Liloc, is a fan of the movie Lilo & Stitch. Since the owner's surname is Liloc, the name "Lilo(C)" was created by separating the letter C and placing it in parentheses. The capital letter "C" represents Crochet, while "Stitch" represents the careful stitches used to create each handmade product.


Each creation is made to show the beauty of handmade craftsmanship and to bring warmth, creativity, and happiness to our customers. Every piece is carefully made with patience and attention to detail.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create quality handmade crochet products that bring comfort, creativity, and happiness to our customers. We aim to make every product with patience, care, and attention to detail while appreciating the value of handmade craftsmanship.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted handmade crochet studio known for creative designs, quality products, and meaningful creations made with care. We hope to continue growing while sharing the beauty and uniqueness of handmade crochet with more people.

Our Goals

  • Create unique and high-quality crochet products.
  • Provide friendly and reliable service to customers.
  • Continue improving our designs and craftsmanship.
  • Share the beauty of handmade creations with others.
  • Build a trusted and creative handmade brand.
